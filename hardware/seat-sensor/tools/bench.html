1. Board check to do

Connect the board. The page turns on the board's telemetry (tele on) and reads its settings.

–
Try typing it: click the box under the console, type info, press Enter. Same result as the button.

2. Counting both ways to do

Turn the shaft slowly one way, then the other. This passes by itself once the count has moved at least 100 each way.

Up so far: 0 · down so far: 0

Leave direction alone on the bench. Set it on the erg so moving away from the flywheel reads positive.

3. Ten turns out and back to do

Tape a flag on the shaft and line it up with a mark. Each button records the current count.

N0 –, N1 –, N2 –

Pass: N1 − N0 = 10 000 ± 3 (500 PPR × 2 edges × 10 turns), and N2 − N0 within ± 3.

Try typing it: pos prints the same count the buttons record.

4. Speed headroom (optional, drill) to do

Spin the shaft with a drill, in line and with no side load. Watch "max seen" above. Then line the flag up again.

–

Pass: count back within ± 3. Fail at high rpm means the encoder can't keep up: fit a 40T encoder pulley.

5. Span against the tape (optional, belt) to do

Belt teeth-up along the tape, roll the pulley along it. Mark at 100 mm, roll to 600 mm, then save.

distance mm
RunCountsnm/countvs 40 000

Pass: three runs, each within ± 0.5 % of 40 000, and agreeing with each other within 0.2 %. If they don't agree, go back to nominal.

6. Homing logic to do

Use a GND jumper tapped on GPIO 6, or the magnet past the reed. Reed state shows in Live.

7. Bluetooth to do

Click Connect Bluetooth at the top and pick "SplitIQ Seat". Chrome or Edge on Windows, or Chrome on Android. The page reads Info and Status, then subscribes to samples.

–

Pass: Info bytes match, about 25 packets/s, every packet's time stamp steps by exactly 40 000 µs, and BLE position matches USB position.

8. Sleep and wake to do

The board stays awake while this page is connected. To test the 5-minute sleep, disconnect Bluetooth and click Disconnect board, leave the shaft still for 5 minutes, then turn it and reconnect. The wake cause shows in step 1.

currents (mA): adv stream sleep
